## confirm — verb (`office-confirm-arrangement`)

- Definition: to say that an arrangement or detail is correct or definite.
- Vietnamese support: xác nhận.
- Collocations: confirm an appointment; confirm the time.
- Context `appointment-time`: “Please confirm your appointment time by email.”
- Context `delivery-address`: “The customer called to confirm the delivery address.”
- Definition match: “Please confirm your appointment time by email. What does ‘confirm’ mean?”
  - Correct: state that the time is definite.
  - Distractors: cancel the appointment; move it to another day.
  - Explanation: confirming tells the recipient that the stated time is correct or definite.
- Typing cloze: “The delivery address is correct. Please ___ it by email.”
  - Accepted answer: `confirm`.
  - Explanation: confirm means to state that a detail is correct.

## postpone — verb (`office-postpone-meeting`)

- Definition: to arrange for an event to happen later than planned.
- Vietnamese support: hoãn lại.
- Collocations: postpone a meeting; postpone a decision.
- Context `meeting-rescheduled`: “We had to postpone the meeting until Thursday.”
- Context `decision-delayed`: “The committee decided to postpone its decision until next week.”
- Definition match: “We had to postpone the meeting until Thursday. What does ‘postpone’ mean?”
  - Correct: arrange for it to happen later.
  - Distractors: hold it earlier; cancel it permanently.
  - Explanation: postpone changes the event to a later time without necessarily cancelling it.
- Multiple-choice cloze: “The committee needs more time, so it will ___ its decision until next week.”
  - Correct: `postpone`.
  - Distractors: `announce`; `finalize`.
  - Explanation: postponing moves the decision to a later time.

## deadline — noun (`office-deadline-submission`)

- Definition: the latest time or date by which something must be done.
- Vietnamese support: hạn chót.
- Collocations: meet a deadline; submission deadline.
- Context `report-submission`: “The deadline for the report is Friday at noon.”
- Context `application-close`: “The application deadline is 5 p.m. on Monday.”
- Definition match: “The deadline for the report is Friday at noon. What is the deadline?”
  - Correct: the latest time to submit the report.
  - Distractors: the report's title; the person who wrote the report.
  - Explanation: the deadline specifies the latest time for completing or submitting the work.
- Typing cloze: “Applications close at 5 p.m. Monday. That is the submission ___.”
  - Accepted answer: `deadline`.
  - Explanation: a submission deadline is the latest permitted submission time.

## agenda — noun (`office-agenda-meeting`)

- Definition: a list of topics to discuss at a meeting.
- Vietnamese support: chương trình họp.
- Collocations: meeting agenda; add an item to the agenda.
- Context `team-meeting-topics`: “The agenda lists three topics for tomorrow’s team meeting.”
- Context `client-discussion`: “Please add the budget review to the client meeting agenda.”
- Definition match: “The agenda lists three topics for tomorrow’s team meeting. What is the agenda?”
  - Correct: the list of topics for discussion.
  - Distractors: a list of employees' salaries; the room's seating plan.
  - Explanation: a meeting agenda lists the matters participants plan to discuss.
- Multiple-choice cloze: “Please add the budget review to the list of meeting topics, or the ___.”
  - Correct: `agenda`.
  - Distractors: `invoice`; `receipt`.
  - Explanation: an agenda is the list of topics for a meeting.
